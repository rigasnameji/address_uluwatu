01 / 06 Why Families Choose It

Private Enough For Rest, Big Enough For Togetherness

Address Bali Villa is built around the reality of large family travel: grandparents, parents, cousins, teens, children, and friends all need different rhythms. The villa keeps everyone in one private estate, with shared living and dining spaces at the center and en-suite bedrooms giving each family unit a clear place to retreat.

The villa details highlight a fully serviced setup, a large private pool, room to dine and relax as a group, and a Bingin location close to beach access, restaurants, cafes, gyms, yoga studios, spas, and everyday essentials.

02 / 06 Bedroom Flexibility

Rooming That Works For Mixed Families

The sleeping setup makes it easier to host several family units without forcing one rigid layout. Six rooms feature California king beds, while three rooms can be arranged as twins or converted into king beds with a topper.

Every bedroom includes air conditioning, en-suite shower/WC, 65-inch smart TV, mini fridge, Nespresso machine, Dyson hair dryer, natural latex mattress, microfiber pillows, 300-thread-count cotton sheets, and electronic key-card access.
