01 / 07 Why The Villa Works For Retreats

Private Suites, Shared Flow, Walkable Bingin

The villa already reads like a retreat brief: fully serviced, 9 en-suite bedrooms, a large pool, generous indoor-outdoor living, and enough communal space for movement, meals, workshops, and quiet recovery without pushing the group off-site.

For organisers, the layout solves the usual retreat tension between togetherness and privacy. Guests can spend the day in shared practice and dining zones, then step back into key-card-access bedrooms with air-conditioning, smart TVs, mini fridges, and private bathrooms.

  • Flat walk to cafés, restaurants, gyms, yoga studios, spas, and everyday essentials.
  • Large communal area with pool, projector, Sonos sound, pool table, ping pong, and lounge seating.
  • Professional kitchen plus private chef and catering options from 07:00 to 23:00.
  • Villa manager check-in, transport help, and add-on experiences for hosted stays.

Quick Facts

  • Up to 20 guests upon request (2 extra beds available).
  • Average WiFi speed of 50-200 Mbps across the MESH network.
  • Parking for 2 cars and approximately 12 motorbikes.
  • Open space for movement and group sessions.
  • Concierge for surf, wellness, and excursions.
  • Private chef available 07:00-23:00.
  • 24/7 security with a discreet on-site support team in a separate rear staff area.
