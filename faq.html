01 / 04 Fast Answers

The Questions That Usually Decide The Booking

Contact concierge
01

How many guests can stay?

The villa is set up for 18 guests, with 9 en-suite bedrooms, 12 beds, and optional extra beds available for an additional fee. For a complex group split, confirm the final guest count and rooming setup before booking.

02

Are events allowed?

Retreats, birthday celebrations, intimate weddings, and special gatherings are possible with approval. The villa is in a quiet area, so parties, loud music, unapproved visitors, commercial stays, and room resale setups need owner approval before booking.

03

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is best treated as an arranged service unless your booking package says otherwise. Private chef and catering service is available from 07:00 to 23:00, and sample menus or costs can be requested before arrival.

04

Is transport available?

Yes. Airport transfers and local transport can be arranged in advance, and the team can also help with private drivers, speedboat trips, helicopter tours, day excursions, surf lessons, wellness treatments, and other Bali experiences through the guest app after booking.

05

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms are confirmed with your booking details and dates. Ask the concierge before confirming, especially for events, peak-season trips, and large groups with multiple guests coordinating travel.

06

Is daily housekeeping included?

The villa is fully serviced, with a villa manager check-in, discreet onsite support, 24/7 security, and routine outdoor maintenance. Confirm the preferred housekeeping rhythm for your stay so the team can align around your group schedule.

07

What time is check-in and checkout?

Check-in is after 3:00 PM and checkout is before 11:00 AM. Checkout is strict so the team can prepare the villa for the next guests. If a late departure is essential, arrange an extra night in advance.

08

Are pets allowed?

Pets may be possible by request. Confirm the pet details before booking so the team can plan for cleaning, safety, and any stay-specific considerations.

09

Can guests cook, or is a chef required?

Guests have access to the kitchen and the listing notes a professional-grade setup with a large oven, separate griddle, grill/BBQ, and two fridges. Chef and catering service can be added when you want the hosted dining experience.

10

How close is the villa to the beach and restaurants?

The listing places the villa less than 300 meters from the Bingin Beach stairs, with cafes, restaurants, gyms, yoga venues, laundry, spas, and everyday essentials within a short walk.

02 / 04 Stay Details

Important Notes That Prevent Surprises

Children

Kids are welcome, supervision matters

The pool and pond are unfenced, and some architectural features may not suit unsupervised children. A bamboo safety fence can be arranged on request for an added rental fee.

Visitors

Only booked guests are automatically approved

Extra visitors require prior approval from the owner. This is especially important for celebrations, photo/video shoots, retreats, and gatherings with external vendors.

Connectivity

WiFi is strongest in shared areas

The MESH network averages 50-200 Mbps in shared areas. Portable WiFi devices are available when guests need a dedicated connection for calls, remote work, or streaming.

Privacy

There is discreet onsite support

A small onsite team stays in a separate rear staff area and may access outdoor spaces for routine maintenance. Guests who need complete isolation should confirm expectations before booking.
