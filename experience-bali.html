Rp. 4.750.000 BEST RATE GUARANTEED

Chase some big fish in the offshore with heavy-duty equipment. Test your luck and bring Giant Trevally, Dogtooth Tuna, and many more within your grip. Choose the technique that you prefer the most.

  • Trolling: Lure the bait into the ocean, tie them behind the boat as the boat roams around the sea
  • Jigging: Throw the jig deep into the ocean until a certain depth. Move the jig up and down to attract the fish
  • Popping: Use big popper on the surface of the sea. Pull the string firmly to get the fish
  • Bottom Fishing: Use fresh bait to attract the fish. Throw it to a certain depth and bring some big fish home
