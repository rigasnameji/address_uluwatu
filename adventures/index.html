07 / 07 FAQ

Before You Book

Can everything be arranged after we arrive?

Yes, but premium experiences such as boats, helicopters, ceremonies, and private dining are best requested early so the concierge can confirm trusted partners.

Do we pay online?

No. This first version keeps the booking flow simple: send an inquiry, then the concierge confirms availability, inclusions, and payment details manually.

Can experiences be customized for groups?

Yes. Most activities can be shaped around your group size, villa schedule, wellness plans, dining preferences, and transport needs.

Are prices final?

Some prices are fixed starting rates, while others depend on treatment, destination, route, guest count, timing, and partner availability.
