Built For Big Groups Without Losing Privacy

Address Bali Villa works for larger groups because the whole stay can happen inside one private estate: private en-suite bedrooms for downtime, shared living and dining areas for everyone to come together, and a walkable Bingin location close to the beach stairs, cafés, restaurants, gyms, yoga studios, spas, and everyday essentials.

The Airbnb listing positions the villa for large families, groups of friends, retreats, birthday celebrations, intimate weddings, and special gatherings. It is best for hosted, pre-arranged occasions — not loud parties or unapproved visitors.

02 / 05 Sleeping Capacity

Flexible Rooming For Couples, Singles, And Teams

The villa sleeps 18 guests across 9 en-suite bedrooms and 12 beds, with 2 extra beds available on request. Six bedrooms feature California king beds, while three rooms can be arranged as twins or converted into king beds with a topper, giving planners useful flexibility for mixed groups.

Each bedroom includes air conditioning, en-suite shower/WC, 65-inch smart TV, mini fridge, Nespresso machine, Dyson hair dryer, natural latex mattress, microfiber pillows, 300-thread-count cotton sheets, and electronic key-card access.
