02 / 07 Villa Description

Designed For Shared Luxury

Address Bali Villa sits in the center of Bingin, less than a 300-metre flat walk from the beach stairs, cafés, restaurants, gyms, yoga studios, spas, and the everyday essentials that make longer stays feel easy. The draw here is not just size, but flow: the property is arranged so big groups can spend the day together without losing the comfort of private rooms.

The communal heart of the estate is built around a large private swimming pool with a shallow shelf, generous dining and lounge zones, and enough activity built in to keep the villa feeling lively without needing to leave. The villa highlights pool volleyball, an outdoor cinema projector with Sonos sound, a full-size pool table, ping pong, board-game zones, yoga equipment, and a professional-grade kitchen with a large oven, separate griddle, and grill/BBQ.

It is also especially strong for wellness-focused groups. There is clear space for meditation, yoga, and group exercise sessions, plus a gym / fitness room, yoga space, massage table, spa support, and private or group training options that make retreat-style programming easy to run on site.

For hosted stays, the villa is set up to go beyond accommodation. Private chef and catering service runs from 07:00 to 23:00, and the team can arrange masseurs, housekeeping, concierge services, chauffeurs, airport transfers, surf, yoga, personal training, wellness treatments, private drivers, boat trips, speedboat excursions, helicopter tours, and custom island itineraries.

  • Guest favourite stay with a 4.91/5 rating from 64 reviews.
